Souvenir sticker, 50 × 30 mm, 30 per A4 sheet. In the print dialog choose A4, Landscape, margins None, scale 100%, and turn on Background graphics. Print on A4 sticker paper (matte or semi-gloss), then cut along the crop marks.

Single sticker

A4 landscape print sheet

Printing instructions

  1. Press Print / Save as PDF.
  2. Paper A4, orientation Landscape, scale 100% (not “fit to page”), margins None, Background graphics on.
  3. Print one test page on plain paper first and hold it against a ruler: each sticker should measure 50 × 30 mm.
  4. Cut along the thin crop marks; the grey outline shows each sticker’s edge.

Adjust layout (mm)

These settings do not fit on the A4 sheet.
