Our Wedding Invitation Set

Two cards. Two sets. One A4 sheet.

How to print and cut (1 minute)
  1. Click PRINT SHEET. Choose paper A4, orientation Portrait, margins None, scale 100%, and tick Background graphics.
  2. Print both pages on one sheet, double-sided, flipping on the long edge. Page 1 = main-card fronts (top) and information cards (bottom). Page 2 = main-card backs (top).
  3. No duplex printer? Print page 1, put the sheet back so the printed side faces the right way, then print page 2 only.
  4. Cut along the crop marks (the small black ticks outside each corner). Cut the row gap first, then separate the cards.
  5. Each guest gets one main card and one information card, tucked together.

Test on plain paper first and hold it up to the light to check the front/back alignment. Home printers usually cannot print closer than 3–5 mm to the edge. The crop marks stay inside 5 mm.
