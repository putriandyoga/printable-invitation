Pen souvenir backing card, 55 × 180 mm, 5 per A4 landscape. Print at A4 Landscape, margins None, scale 100%, Background graphics on. Cut the card outline, then cut the slit lines; the pen slides under the two paper bands.

Printing instructions

  1. Press Print / Save as PDF, paper A4, Landscape, margins None, scale 100%, Background graphics on.
  2. Print on 250–300 gsm matte card stock.
  3. Cut along the crop marks to 55 × 180 mm.
  4. Cut the four thin slit lines on each card with a craft knife.
  5. Slide the pen under the two paper bands between each pair of slits.

Adjust to your pen (mm)

Defaults suit a slim pen about 10 mm wide. More options are in the CFG object in the script.
