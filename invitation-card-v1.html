How to print (30 seconds)
  1. Click PRINT INVITATION. Choose paper A4, orientation Landscape, margins None, scale 100%, and tick Background graphics.
  2. Print both pages double-sided, flipping on the short edge. Page 1 holds the two fronts, page 2 the two backs.
  3. No duplex printer? Print page 1, put the sheet back so the printed side faces the right way, then print page 2 only.
  4. Cut the sheet along the dashed line at the centre. You get two flat A5 invitations (no folding); the guest-label side is the front.

Always test on plain paper first to check the front/back alignment and that the back is not upside down. Most home printers cannot print right to the edge, so a thin white border is normal. Turn on "Safe-area guides" to see where it is safe.
