How to print (30 seconds)
  1. Click PRINT INVITATION. Choose paper A4, orientation Landscape, margins None, scale 100%, and tick Background graphics.
  2. Print both pages double-sided, flipping on the short edge. Page 1 is the outside, page 2 is the inside.
  3. No duplex printer? Print page 1, put the sheet back so the printed side faces the right way, then print page 2 only.
  4. Fold the sheet in half along the centre: the names go on the front.

Always test on plain paper first to check the front/back alignment and fold direction. Most home printers cannot print right to the edge, so a thin white border is normal. Turn on "Safe-area guides" to see where it is safe.
